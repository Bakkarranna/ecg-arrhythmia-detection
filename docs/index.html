What this does not show

A results page that only lists wins is not a results page. These are the numbers the artefacts give up when read carefully.

  • F is the failure mode. F1 0.69% on 388 beats. A class this rare is easy to lose inside a weighted loss; the honest reading is that the pipeline does not solve F, it solves the three classes that carry the volume.
  • Precision is bought with S. N precision 97.49% forces S precision to 3.21% - 7,594 true N beats in the baseline confusion matrix come from that trade. For screening that is the right direction; for per-beat annotation it is not.
  • Synthetic pipeline, real benchmark. The beat windowing, AAMI mapping and split follow de Chazal et al. (2006) and are re-implemented here, not imported. The RandomForest baseline exists to show the shallow model's ceiling, not to beat it.
  • Beats, not rhythms. Every sample is an independently labelled 252-sample window. Atrial fibrillation, the arrhythmia clinicians care most about, is a rhythm-level property and is invisible to this input representation.
